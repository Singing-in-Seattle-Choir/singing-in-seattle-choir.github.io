About us

Music that brings people together

Singing in Seattle Choir is a nonprofit founded on the belief that music can unite people across cultures and inspire positive change. Our singers come from many backgrounds, and our repertoire celebrates international music, with performances meant to inspire, educate and entertain.

Our story

Singing together since 2014

Singing in Seattle Choir was founded in 2014. In 2025 we became a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and some of our original singers are still with us today. Over the years the choir has grown from 10 singers to almost 40, while keeping the warm, welcoming spirit it started with.

Our values

What we stand for

Everyone is welcome, regardless of background or experience. We value every member’s voice and story.

We are a supportive community. Every rehearsal and performance is a chance to connect and grow together.
