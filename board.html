About us

Board of Directors

The volunteers who lead the choir, make the music happen and keep our nonprofit running.

Our story & missionBoard of DirectorsTransparency

Alyona Lvova

Music Director & Conductor

Has led the choir since its foundation. Alyona brings out the best in every singer, from professionals to first-timers, pairing technical skill with emotional expression in a supportive rehearsal room.

Artem Livshits

Executive Director

A tenor and the choir’s sound operator, Artem has sung with Singing in Seattle since its foundation, including through the pandemic. He helped lead the choir to 501(c)(3) status and works to grow its reputation and community impact.

Lucy Raikova

Director of Technology & Communications

An alto who leads the choir’s technology and communications, building an inclusive, collaborative community and keeping members, volunteers and supporters connected.

Iouliana Stadnikova

Finance Director & Treasurer

An alto with decades of experience in data analytics, financial planning and management. Iouliana helped the choir earn charitable nonprofit status and works to strengthen its finances and the trust of its supporters.

Kirill Kolyshkin

Secretary

A bass who has sung with the choir for four years. Kirill loves its varied repertoire across languages and styles, and supports the choir’s operations as it grows.
